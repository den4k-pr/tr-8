Faq

How long is the course access?
You get 3 months of access. You can complete the course within this time or repurchase access if needed.
What is Karyna's secret method?
It's a dynamic stretching method Karyna built over 12+ years. You stay in movement, so your muscles warm up and stretch deeper without painful holds. Both new 2026 courses are built on it.
Is this course suitable for beginners?
Absolutely! Whether you're just starting or have been working on flexibility for a while, this course is designed for all levels. You'll get step-by-step guidance, so you'll feel confident every step of the way, even if you're new to flexibility training.
What if I don't get access to the course right away?
If there's any issue, we'll fix it immediately! Simply reach out to our support team, and we'll ensure you get access within 24 hours. We're here to make sure you can start training right away!
Will I have personal support throughout the course?
Yes! We offer ongoing support and are here to guide you on your journey. If you have questions, need advice, or want to share your progress, our team is always available to help you stay on track and motivated.
How long will it take for me to see results?
Flexibility is a journey. With consistent practice, you'll start seeing noticeable improvements in just 2-4 weeks. If you stick with it, your progress will grow faster, and soon you'll be hitting new goals!
Do I need special equipment for the course?
The only equipment you need is a yoga mat and blocks. This course is designed to be done at home, without the need for fancy gym gear. You can practice in the comfort of your own space, making it easy and convenient to get started!
